About Us

No Money → Have Money is a demo loan app front‑end designed to showcase how instant loan platforms work. We focus on simplicity, transparency, and speed — helping users understand the flow from application to disbursal.

  • 📄 Easy registration and login
  • ⚡ Instant loan application
  • 🔍 KYC verification (demo only)
  • 📊 Loan calculator with EMI preview
  • 📱 Dashboard to track loan status

KYC & Instant Loan

For real loan apps, KYC (Know Your Customer) verification is mandatory. This demo simulates KYC checks and instant loan disbursal to help you visualize the process. In production, this would involve secure identity verification and compliance with financial regulations.
